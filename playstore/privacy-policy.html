Renewly Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 May 2026

Renewly is a policy, warranty, and service reminder app for vehicles and gadgets. This privacy policy explains how Renewly handles information in the current prototype version.

Information You Add

You may choose to add vehicle details, device details, warranty dates, policy dates, service dates, provider or seller names, owner names, amounts, notes, and document references such as bills, receipts, policies, or warranty cards.

Local Storage

In the current prototype, Renewly stores your records locally on your device. Renewly does not currently provide cloud sync, account login, or server-side storage.

Notifications

Renewly may ask for notification permission so it can remind you about upcoming renewal, warranty, insurance, PUC, or service dates. Notifications are used only for app reminders.

Data Sharing

Renewly does not sell your data. In the current prototype, Renewly does not share your records with third parties.

Ads and Analytics

The current prototype does not include advertising SDKs or analytics SDKs. If ads, analytics, cloud backup, or account features are added later, this policy will be updated before release.

External Links

Future versions may include links to official renewal websites or provider websites. Renewly is an independent reminder app and is not affiliated with insurers, banks, brands, service providers, or government agencies unless clearly stated.

Your Control

You can edit, archive, delete, or export your app records from within Renewly. You can also remove local app data by uninstalling the app or clearing app storage from your device settings.
