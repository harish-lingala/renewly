# Renewly Store Listing

## App Name

Renewly

## Short Description

Track warranty, insurance, and service expiry dates for vehicles and gadgets.

## Full Description

Renewly helps you remember important renewal and service dates for your vehicles and gadgets.

Add automobile records such as insurance, PUC, warranty, battery warranty, tyre warranty, and service due dates. Add electronics records such as mobile, laptop, appliance warranty, service contract, and purchase bill details.

Renewly keeps your renewal information organized in one simple dashboard so you can see what is upcoming, due this week, overdue, or service due.

Key features:

- Track automobile and electronics renewal dates
- Add warranty, insurance, PUC, and service reminders
- Save provider, seller, amount, notes, and reference details
- Attach bill, warranty, policy, or receipt references
- View upcoming reminders on dashboard and calendar
- Keep renewal and service history for comparison
- Use documents view to find policy or warranty records quickly
- Get local notification reminders before important dates
- Export backup data from the app

Renewly is currently focused on automobiles and electronics. More categories such as home appliances, health insurance, life insurance, subscriptions, licences, and family document tracking may be added in future versions.

Renewly is an independent reminder and organizer app. It is not an insurance provider, broker, financial advisor, vehicle brand, electronics brand, or government service.
